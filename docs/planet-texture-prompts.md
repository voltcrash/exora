# Exora Chemistry Texture Prompts

These project assets were generated with OpenAI's built-in image generation tool. Every prompt
used the `stylized-concept` use case and requested a seamless square color-detail texture for a
real-time 3D exoplanet shader.

Shared production constraints:

> Material fills the frame edge to edge; perfectly orthographic top-down; uniform scale; seamless
> and tileable on every edge; physically plausible high-resolution PBR albedo; flat diffuse
> capture with no directional shadows, highlights, or baked illumination; homogeneous detail
> density; no planet, sphere, crater, horizon, object, text, watermark, vignette, perspective,
> obvious repeated pattern, or large isolated feature.

Per-material prompts:

- **Oxidized:** iron-rich alien desert surface with deep hematite red, burnt sienna, muted rust,
  sparse charcoal grains, fine ferric dust, wind-polished grains, thin desiccation veins, and
  subtle layered mineral variation.
- **Sulfuric:** sulfur-rich mineral surface with ochre, muted sulfur yellow, pale mineral salts,
  sparse dark volcanic inclusions, powdery deposits, fine crystalline crust, tiny fractures, and
  millimeter-to-centimeter grain variation.
- **Carbon:** carbon-rich alien basalt with nearly black graphite, blue-black volcanic glass,
  dark slate, sparse cool-gray flecks, fine carbonaceous regolith, microfractured glass, compacted
  dust, and subtle layered grain.
- **Silicate:** neutral silicate alien regolith with cool-gray stone dust, muted taupe grains, pale
  feldspar flecks, sparse dark inclusions, powdered stone, tiny angular grains, and subtle mineral
  speckling.
- **Ice:** ancient alien water ice with blue-white compacted ice, translucent gray fissures, pale
  frost grains, subtle frozen mineral inclusions, crystalline ice, thin stress fractures, and
  embedded salts.
